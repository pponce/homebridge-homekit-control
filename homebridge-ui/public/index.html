HomeKit Control

Connect Wi-Fi HomeKit accessories to Homebridge. Devices keep their original firmware.

Add a device

Devices must be on Wi-Fi and available to pair. If already in Apple Home, remove their direct pairing first. Homebridge will expose the device back to Apple Home; room and automation assignments may need updating.

Pairing keys are saved privately on the Homebridge server. Restart Homebridge after adding a device.
